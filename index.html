Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a measurement calculated using height and weight.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

Is BMI a complete health measurement?

No. BMI is only a screening measurement and does not provide a complete picture of health.

Can teenagers use BMI?

Yes, but BMI for teenagers must be interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts.
